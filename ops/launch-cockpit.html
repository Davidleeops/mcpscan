Current Reality

MCPScan is best sold as a pre-enablement MCP readiness audit for teams turning on Copilot, Claude Code, Cursor, VS Code agent mode, Slack MCP, or Atlassian Rovo. The first revenue motion is a fixed-scope paid audit, not a full SaaS platform.

First Revenue Runway

After domain, mailbox, Stripe, and security contact are live, open the public, outbound, reply, payment, and delivery approval surfaces from one command.

npm run launch:open-first-revenue

Hard Rules

  • No external messages without same-turn approval of exact recipient and content.
  • No customer secrets or reports in the public repo.
  • No outbound from the new mailbox until MX, SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass.
  • No npm publish until login/2FA is confirmed.
