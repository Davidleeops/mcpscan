Never Skip

  • No external messages without same-turn approval of exact recipient and content.
  • No customer secrets, private configs, customer data, or final reports in the public repo.
  • No outbound from the new mailbox until MX, SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass.
  • No live delivery until payment and safe intake are confirmed.
