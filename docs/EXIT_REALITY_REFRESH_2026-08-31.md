## What This Means For A Quick Exit

The good news: acquisition appetite exists around agent security, AI development governance, testing, runtime assurance, and MCP risk.

The hard news: buyers will not pay strategic money for a thin wrapper around commodity MCP scanning. The acquirable asset needs one of these:

1. Evidence workflow: repeatable reports, proof trails, QA gates, and remediation artifacts that security teams can hand to leadership or customers.
2. Proprietary data: anonymized benchmark patterns from real MCP and agent-tool audits.
3. Distribution wedge: a trusted first-revenue motion among AI app builders, agent startups, and platform teams.
4. Delivery machine: the ability to turn paid audits into reusable checks and product improvements.
5. Integration path: fit with Snyk, OpenAI, Anaconda, Cisco, Proofpoint, Wiz, Palo Alto, Noma, Zenity, or developer-platform ecosystems.
